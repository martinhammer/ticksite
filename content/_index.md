# Hello!

Tickbuddy is a daily habit and event tracker for Nextcloud.

Tickbuddy lets you keep track of what happens each day, such as your meditation, exercise, or how many cups of coffee you had. Your scripts and AI agents can also log automatically, for example to record nightly backups. Tickbuddy is flexible: use it for habits, chores, keeping an eye on automations, or anything else that matters to you. Over time, analyse patterns and statistics with the built-in analytics.

It is **fully open source**, **respects your privacy**, **doesn't lock in your data** and can easily be **self-hosted**. It is simple to use, yet powerful and flexible. Tickbuddy and its companion apps support logging and tracking via web, mobile, command line, MCP and APIs.

![Pencil sketch of the Tickbuddy ecosystem: Tickbuddy on your Nextcloud server, with the web UI, Tickdroid, Tickcli, TickLLMe and ping URLs around it](/images/tickbuddy-napkin-1200.webp)

Tickbuddy itself is a Nextcloud application which provides the common backend and APIs, as well as the web UI.
- [Tickbuddy](https://github.com/martinhammer/tickbuddy) is published on the [Nextcloud app store](https://apps.nextcloud.com/apps/tickbuddy)
- [Tickdroid](https://github.com/martinhammer/tickdroid), the Android app, is available on [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.martinhammer.tickdroid/)
- [Tickcli](https://github.com/martinhammer/tickcli) is the command-line client for scripts and automations
- [TickLLMe](https://github.com/martinhammer/tickllme) is an MCP server that lets AI agents interact with Tickbuddy
